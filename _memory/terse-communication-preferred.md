Answer in as few words as possible. No long explanatory blocks, no tables unless they
genuinely compress, no restating context he already has. Lead with the answer.

**Why:** Asked directly on 2026-09-09 after I sent several multi-paragraph explanations
during the visual-identity plan 1 execution — "I am not in the mood to read blocks of text".

**How to apply:** Default to 1-3 sentences. Expand only when he asks, or when a decision
genuinely needs the reasoning. Status updates: bullets, not prose. Keep [[open-a-file-means-open-it]]
style directness.
